Privacy Policy

ClipPanel browser extension · Last updated: 2026-08-06

ClipPanel is an independent extension for saving playable videos from vimeo.com pages. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Vimeo, Inc. This privacy policy describes how the extension handles information. The extension is designed to work locally in your browser.

1. Summary

  • No account is required.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives your videos.
  • Download and merge happen on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data.

2. Data the extension accesses

Data Purpose Stored where
Media metadata on vimeo.com pages (title, thumbnail, qualities, playlists) Detect downloadable videos On-device memory / chrome.storage
Download history Show history in the side panel chrome.storage.local
Settings Remember preferences chrome.storage.local
Temporary HLS segments during merge Build a single MP4 On-device temporary storage, then removed when possible

3. Data we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect or send to our servers: account contents, payment data, precise location, unrelated browsing history, analytics/ad IDs, or your finished video file (it is saved via the browser Downloads folder). This extension does not include third-party analytics or ad SDKs.

4. Permissions

Tabs, navigation, scripting, and side panel APIs power detection and UI. Downloads saves the file. Storage holds settings/history/temp merge data. Offscreen documents run local remux. Host permissions cover vimeo.com and its media CDNs only for fetching media you requested.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Network requests go to vimeo.com and related CDNs only as needed for download.

6. Retention & choices

History and settings stay on your device until you clear them or uninstall. You can clear history in the side panel. Files already saved to Downloads remain unless you delete them yourself.

7. Contact

Use the support email or URL shown on the Chrome Web Store / Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing for privacy questions.
