Privacy Policy

TG Media Saver (Chrome / Edge extension)
Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TG Media Saver (“the Extension”) handles information when you use it on Telegram Web (web.telegram.org and related Telegram Web hosts).

1. Overview

The Extension helps you save media (videos, photos, GIFs, and audio) that you can already view in Telegram Web. It runs in your browser and is designed to keep processing on your device.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require your Telegram password or account credentials.
  • We do not operate a developer backend that receives your chat content, contacts, or downloaded files.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the Extension.

3. Information processed on your device

Depending on how you use the Extension, it may process locally:

  • Media URLs and file metadata needed to start a download (for example file name, type, and size when available).
  • Download job status shown in the side panel (active / completed / cancelled).
  • Settings and recent download history stored with browser extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device.
  • Page content on Telegram Web only as needed to find media elements and inject save controls.

Downloaded files are saved through your browser’s download manager to the location you choose on your computer.

4. Permissions

The Extension may request these browser permissions:

  • Host access to Telegram Web — detect media and show save buttons on Telegram Web pages.
  • storage — remember settings and local history.
  • tabs — communicate with the active Telegram tab.
  • downloads — save files with the browser download API when appropriate.
  • sidePanel — show download progress and settings.
  • scripting — inject the on-page UI used for saving media.

5. Third-party services

Telegram Web is operated by Telegram. Your use of Telegram is governed by Telegram’s own terms and privacy policy. The Extension only helps you save media you can already access while signed in to Telegram in your browser.

6. Data retention

Local settings and history remain on your device until you clear extension data, remove the Extension, or clear browser storage. We do not maintain a remote copy of that data.

7. Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Extension after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, use the support / contact options listed on the extension’s Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons listing page.
